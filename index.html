FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

A few useful answers before you get started.

Is Assessly a learning course?

No. Assessly is our school assessment tool. Our hands-on digital skills classes are a separate part of SpotLight.

What can our school manage with Assessly?

Student and class records, question banks, tests, exams and results are brought into a shared assessment workflow.

Can we discuss our school’s assessment needs first?

Yes. Use the enquiry form to describe your school and the assessment tasks you want to organise. Avoid sharing student records in your message.

Where should we start?

Enquire about Assessly with your school name, your role and a short outline of your current process. We can discuss a suitable next step.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

A few useful answers before you get started.

Do we need an existing coding programme?

No existing programme is needed to begin a conversation. Tell us what your school already offers and what your students are ready to explore.

Can a pathway suit different age groups?

Yes. Project scope, pace and activities can be discussed around primary or secondary learners and their current experience.

What equipment should we prepare?

Share the devices, connectivity and teaching space you have. We will discuss practical requirements before a starting plan is agreed.

Can we ask about Assessly as well?

Yes. Mention Assessly in your enquiry if you also want to discuss students, classes, question banks, exams and results.
